DATA & METHODOLOGY

Research with the full context.

This site implements the workflow described in DSO-576.pdf using the public project repository’s cleaned tables: 2,656 daily price records, 3,888 news summaries, and 73 filing excerpts across eight companies.

What gets flagged?

Daily range is (high − low) ÷ close × 100. Top 5% uses the 95th percentile of each company’s full available history (linear interpolation), before filtering by year. This makes volatility comparable across stocks with different price levels. A wide range is not the same as a large close-to-close return.

How context is matched

News covers the selected calendar day and previous seven calendar days. Filings use the latest dated record on or before the selection, including earlier years. Same-day filings may have been released after trading hours. If a quarterly risk section refers to a 10-K, the latest available annual report is also shown.

Quick summaries

Quick takeaways automatically select a few complete sentences from the dataset text, grouped by topic. They are extractive summaries, rather than independently verified analysis, and may omit important context. Expand the full dataset text to review the source. A missing topic means no suitable sentence was found, not that the topic is absent.

Coverage & limits

Prices and news span December 2024 through March 2026; some years are partial. News is AI-summarized and may contain errors; original article URLs are not supplied. Filings contain MD&A and risk excerpts, rather than complete SEC documents. Co-flags are a partial check for shared movements, not proof of a market-wide cause. Prices shown are unadjusted OHLC values; corporate actions can affect comparisons.

Shortlists stay in this browser. Export them to retain a copy. This research tool provides neither predictions nor investment recommendations.
