DATA & METHODOLOGY

How to read this dashboard

The full cleaned Herculean tables are used, with no sampling: 2,656 daily price records, 3,888 news summaries and 73 filing excerpts for eight companies, December 2024 to March 2026. Every figure comes from the project's tested Python backend.

What gets flagged?

Daily range is (high − low) ÷ close. A day is flagged when its range is at or above the 95th percentile of that company's own full history (linear interpolation). That gives 17 flagged days per company. The cutoff is fixed: changing the period never changes which days are flagged. Because it uses the full history, including later days, it is a look back and not a real-time signal. A wide range is not the same as a large close-to-close return.

How context is matched

News covers the selected calendar day and the previous seven calendar days. The filing is the latest one dated on or before the selected day, which can be weeks or months old, so its age is shown. A same-day filing may have been released after trading hours. If a quarterly risk section is labelled "refers to 10-K", the latest annual report's risks are also shown.

Selected sentences

The highlighted sentences are picked automatically from the source text by topic keywords. They are not a summary and are not verified, and they may leave out important context. Open the full text to review the source. A missing topic means no suitable sentence was found, not that the topic is absent.

Limits

There is no market index, so co-flags are only a partial check for shared movement. News is AI-summarized, may contain errors, and has no publication times or article links. Filings are MD&A and risk excerpts, not complete documents. Relative performance uses adjusted prices; the close, high and low shown are unadjusted. This tool gives neither predictions nor investment recommendations.
